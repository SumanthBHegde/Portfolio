## The problem

A directory is only as good as its content, and only as trustworthy as its
editorial control. Open it up completely and quality falls apart. Lock it down
and it never grows. The interesting part is the middle: let a lot of people
contribute, but make sure a human approves things before they go public.

## What I built

Sanjeevini is a full-stack medicinal-plants directory built around governance,
not just pages.

- Three-tier RBAC across User, Editor and Admin, each with its own permissions.
- A content-approval workflow, so submissions move through review before they go
  live.
- Structured content managed in Sanity CMS, rendered by Next.js 15, TypeScript
  and Tailwind.

## How it turned out

It is deployed, and it is the project where contribution and control actually
coexist. That role and workflow modelling is the kind of thing real content
platforms need, and it is one of my larger, more modern codebases.
