## The problem

Heritage sites lose something on a screen. Most virtual tours are a row of
photos. They do not let you look around, move through a space, or learn the way
being there does. We wanted something closer to actually being present.

## What we built

Prapti is an immersive AR/VR heritage platform I built with one teammate.

- An interactive 3D model viewer, using Three.js and WebXR, to inspect artefacts
  and structures.
- 360 degree panoramas so you get the feeling of standing inside a place.
- Google Maps for geographic and cultural context.
- Gamified trivia so exploring teaches you something, not just shows you.
- A real backend and admin on Next.js, Prisma and Neon Postgres, with auth. Not
  a static demo.

## How it turned out

It won the IIC Regional Ideathon 2024. It is also the largest and most complete
codebase I have shipped, with a modern XR stack end to end. It is the project
that convinced me I can take an ambitious, multi-part product from an idea to
something that actually works and holds up in front of judges.
